🚀 START HERE: What Should I Do?

A mechanical 9-step daily routine and binary decision flowchart for disciplined swing trading

⏰
1. Automated Scan Runs 4:15 PM IST

GitHub Actions runs automatically Monday through Friday. It scans all liquid NIFTY Midcap 100 stocks and sends alert messages to your Telegram bot.

🌐
2. Open Dashboard Terminal 4:20 PM IST

Open this dashboard on your browser. Enter your password to load the private terminal with freshly published results.

🚦
3. Check Market Badge (O'Neil M-Rule) Immediate

Look at the top bar. If Market: Uptrend (Green), buying is allowed. If Market: Defensive (Red), stop here — do not trade! Cash is an active position.

🎯
4. Filter for Grade A / A+ Setups Near Buy Tab

Focus strictly on candidates with Grade A+ (Score ≥80) or Grade A (Score ≥70) whose sector index is also in an active Stage 2 uptrend. Ignore Grade B and C.

📐
5. Size with Position Sizer 1 Click

Click "Size this trade" on the chosen stock row. The system switches to the Open Positions tab and auto-computes exact suggested units based on 1% portfolio risk budget (₹1,000 on ₹1 Lakh).

💬
6. Copy & Send Telegram /buy Command 1 Click

Click "📋 Copy /buy command" and paste into your Telegram bot chat. The bot records entry price, quantity, stop loss, and 2R target in open_positions.json.

🛡️
7. Manage Mechanically Next Sessions

Place your Stop-Loss GTT order on your broker terminal. If profit reaches +8%, immediately ratchet stop loss to entry (breakeven). If trade makes zero progress in 5 trading days, cut it via the time stop.

8. Log Exits with /sell On Exit

When either the 2R target or the stop loss is triggered, send /sell SYMBOL EXIT_PRICE on Telegram. The trade moves from open positions to closed trade history with PnL and return %.

📊
9. Review Stats Weekly Every Weekend

Open the Performance Stats tab to review win rate (target: 45-55%), profit factor (target: >1.5), expectancy (target: >₹500 per trade), and equity curve progression.

📚 INDICATOR GLOSSARY (All 39 Metrics)

Plain-English meanings, swing trading importance, exact thresholds from main.py, and practical examples

SMA 50Trend Indicator

The average closing price over the last 50 trading sessions (roughly 10 calendar weeks).

Why it matters:Serves as institutional support during uptrends. Strong swing winners rarely break below a rising 50 SMA.
main.py Threshold:Close > SMA50 and SMA50 > SMA150
Example:If 50-day average is ₹500, stock trading at ₹540 confirms healthy institutional accumulation.
SMA 150Intermediate Trend

The average closing price over 150 trading days (roughly 7 months).

Why it matters:Validates intermediate-term trend structure between the 50-day and 200-day moving averages.
main.py Threshold:SMA150 > SMA200 and Close > SMA150
Example:SMA150 at ₹460 cleanly above SMA200 at ₹430 confirms a sustained Stage 2 cycle.
SMA 200Long-Term Trend

The average closing price over 200 trading days (roughly 10 months). The premier long-term institutional line.

Why it matters:Separates bull markets from bear markets. Never buy swing trades below a falling 200-day SMA.
main.py Threshold:SMA200 > SMA200[50 days ago] (Must be trending up for ≥1 month)
Example:SMA200 today is ₹420 vs ₹400 fifty sessions ago: confirms ascending macro support.
EMA 10Short-Term Momentum

10-period Exponential Moving Average, weighted heavily toward the most recent sessions.

Why it matters:Used for our trailing exit rule once a winning position exceeds its initial target.
main.py Threshold:Trailing Stop: Exit if daily Close < EMA10
Example:Stock climbs from ₹1,000 to ₹1,200; we hold until a daily candle closes below the 10 EMA.
EMA 20Base Support

20-period Exponential Moving Average capturing the 1-month short-term trend speed.

Why it matters:Leading stocks in strong momentum pull back to test their 20 EMA before breaking out to new highs.
main.py Threshold:Used in Bollinger Bands centerline and Keltner Squeeze calculations
Example:Stock consolidates tightly around its 20 EMA while volume contracts.
ATR 14Volatility Gauge

14-day Average True Range. Measures the average rupee movement of a stock per session including gaps.

Why it matters:Allows dynamic stop loss setting based on actual volatility rather than arbitrary percentages.
main.py Threshold:ATR_STOP_MULTIPLIER = 1.5 (Stop = Entry - 1.5 × ATR14)
Example:For ₹1,000 stock with ATR14 of ₹20, initial stop is placed at ₹970 (1.5 × 20 = ₹30 below entry).
RSI 14Momentum Filter

Standard 14-day Relative Strength Index oscillator bounded between 0 and 100.

Why it matters:Ensures we buy stocks with healthy momentum without entering at overheated/exhaustion tops.
main.py Threshold:40.0 <= RSI14 <= 65.0 (+10 Score Points)
Example:RSI14 at 58 indicates positive accumulation without being overbought (>70).
RSI 2Pullback Trigger

Ultra-fast 2-period RSI popularized by Larry Connors to identify short-term rubber-band pullbacks.

Why it matters:Catches institutional dips in ongoing uptrends for high-reward pullback entries.
main.py Threshold:RSI2 < 10 (Dip) then bouncing with Close > Close[1]
Example:Strong uptrend stock drops 2 days, driving RSI2 down to 6.2, then prints a green reversal candle.
Volume Dry-Up (VOL_DRYUP)Supply Contraction

Occurs when trading activity drops significantly during consolidation, proving sellers have dried up.

Why it matters:Without sellers in the order book, it takes minimal institutional demand to propel price upward.
main.py Threshold:VOL5 < 0.80 × VOL50 (5-day avg volume < 80% of 50-day average)
Example:Stock usually trades 1,000,000 shares/day; drops to 600,000 shares/day during tight coiling.
Price Contraction (PRICE_TIGHT)Volatility Squeeze

Daily high-to-low percentage range shrinks dramatically compared to historical norms.

Why it matters:Indicates equilibrium and quiet accumulation before an explosive expansion in volatility.
main.py Threshold:RANGE5 < 0.80 × RANGE50 (5-day candle range < 80% of 50-day range)
Example:Stock daily swings narrow from 4% down to under 1.2% per day.
VCP / Coiling (SETUP_OK)Mark Minervini Pattern

Volatility Contraction Pattern. Simultaneous occurrence of volume dry-up and price range contraction.

Why it matters:The core signature of institutional absorption preceding multi-week breakout moves.
main.py Threshold:VOL_DRYUP & PRICE_TIGHT (+15 Score Points)
Example:Stock prints 3 successive contractions with diminishing volume, tagged as "Coiling: YES".
20-Day Breakout Level (HIGH20)Resistance Line

The highest intra-day price reached across the previous 20 sessions, excluding today's candle.

Why it matters:Represents the ceiling that trapped overhead supply. Crossing it triggers momentum buying.
main.py Threshold:HIGH20 = df["High"].rolling(20).max().shift(1)
Example:Stock hits ceiling at ₹1,250 multiple times; ₹1,250 becomes the breakout trigger line.
Breakout TriggerEntry Signal

Price penetrates and closes above the 20-day high resistance level on heavy volume.

Why it matters:Signals that buyers have absorbed all available supply and are bidding price into new territory.
main.py Threshold:Close > HIGH20 and Close[1] <= HIGH20
Example:Stock crosses ₹1,250 resistance and closes at ₹1,265 on 2.5x volume: generates /buy signal.
Volume Spike (VOL_SPIKE)Confirmation Fuel

Session volume surges well above the 50-day average volume on the day of the breakout.

Why it matters:Distinguishes true institutional buying power from fake retail breakouts.
main.py Threshold:Volume > 1.5 × VOL50 (VOLUME_SPIKE_MULTIPLIER = 1.5)
Example:Stock with 200,000 average volume trades 450,000 shares on breakout day (2.25x spike).
Strong Close (STRONG_CLOSE)Candle Quality

The candle finishes in the top tier of its daily high-to-low trading range.

Why it matters:Filters out intraday rejection wicks and ensure bulls maintained control until the market close.
main.py Threshold:(Close - Low) / (High - Low) > 0.60 (Top 40% of range)
Example:Stock ranges from ₹98 to ₹108 and closes at ₹106: strong close ratio is (106-98)/(108-98) = 0.80.
Sector FilterDouble Confirmation

Requires the individual stock's sector index (e.g. ^CNXIT, ^NSEBANK) to be in an active Stage 2 uptrend.

Why it matters:Prevents buying strong individual stocks swimming upstream against a declining industry group.
main.py Threshold:Close_sector > SMA50_sector > SMA200_sector (+15 Score Points)
Example:COFORGE breakout is confirmed because NIFTY IT (^CNXIT) is in a healthy Stage 2 uptrend.
Market Regime (M-Rule)William O'Neil Rule

Benchmark index filter. 3 out of 4 stocks follow the broader market direction.

Why it matters:In a market correction, even great setups fail at a 75% rate. Staying in cash protects all profits.
main.py Threshold:^NSEI Close > SMA50 > SMA200. Else caps ALL scores at 50!
Example:NIFTY 50 drops below 50 SMA -> Market badge turns Defensive -> new entries blocked.
Distance to BreakoutWatchlist Metric

Percentage difference between the current closing price and the 20-day high resistance level.

Why it matters:Stocks within 0-2% of breakout are prime candidates ready to trigger immediately.
main.py Threshold:dist <= 5.0% for Watchlist; Scale: 20 × max(0, 1 - dist/5)
Example:Stock close is ₹990, 20-day high is ₹1,000 -> Distance is 1.01% away (earns +16 points).
Setup WindowPattern Memory

A memory buffer that keeps a stock eligible for a breakout if it coiled within recent days.

Why it matters:Stocks don't always break out on the exact day they coil; the explosion can occur 1-5 days later.
main.py Threshold:SETUP_WINDOW_DAYS = 10 (10 trading sessions)
Example:Stock coiled 3 sessions ago; today's breakout trigger still qualifies for a valid signal.
1% Risk RuleCapital Preservation

Risking no more than 1% of total portfolio equity on any single swing trade.

Why it matters:Ensures you can survive 10 consecutive losses with your portfolio virtually intact (>90% remaining).
main.py Threshold:CAPITAL = 100000, RISK_PCT = 0.01 (₹1,000 risk budget)
Example:If entry is ₹500 and stop is ₹475 (risk ₹25), Qty = floor(1000 / 25) = 40 shares.
7% Max Stop LossHard Ceiling

An absolute risk floor preventing any trade from having a stop loss wider than 7% from entry.

Why it matters:Wide stops destroy expectancy and require massive recovery gains. Small losses keep you alive.
main.py Threshold:MAX_STOP_LOSS_PCT = 0.07 (stop >= entry × 0.93)
Example:If ATR would place stop at 9% below entry, the code caps the stop at exactly 7% (₹930 on ₹1,000).
2R Target (Asymmetric Reward)Profit Target

Target set at exactly twice the rupee risk taken on the trade.

Why it matters:Allows a swing trader to be profitable with only a 40% win rate (winning ₹2 for every ₹1 lost).
main.py Threshold:TARGET_R_MULTIPLE = 2.0 (Target = Entry + 2 × RiskPerUnit)
Example:Entry ₹1,000, Stop ₹950 (Risk ₹50). Target is ₹1,000 + (2 × 50) = ₹1,100 (+10% gain).
Breakeven Ratchet (+8%)Discipline Rule

Moving the stop loss from the original catastrophic line up to the exact entry price once in solid profit.

Why it matters:Guarantees that a substantial winning trade never turns into a loss. Eliminates downside risk.
main.py Threshold:Discipline rule: Move stop to Entry price when gain reaches +8%
Example:Bought at ₹500, stock rallies to ₹540 (+8%). Stop moved to ₹500 for a free roll.
5-Day Time StopOpportunity Cost

Exiting a breakout trade if the stock makes no upward progress within 5 trading sessions.

Why it matters:True momentum breakouts follow through immediately. Stagnant trades tie up capital and usually fail.
main.py Threshold:Discipline rule: Cut trade after 5 days without progress
Example:Bought breakout on Monday; by next Monday price is hovering flat around entry -> close trade.
Setup Score & GradeComposite Rank

A composite score (0-100) and letter Grade (A+, A, B, C) ranking overall setup perfection.

Why it matters:Filters out the noise and directs your limited capital exclusively to the top 10% of market setups.
main.py Threshold:A+: Score ≥80 & Sector Bullish & Market OK; A: Score ≥70; B: Score ≥60; C: <60
Example:Stock scoring 85 in a confirmed market with a bullish IT sector receives an "A+" green pill.
Volume & LiquidityMarket Activity

The total number of shares transacted during a trading day. Price moves without volume lack true institutional backing.

Why it matters:Ensures institutional participation and prevents slippage when executing swing positions.
main.py Threshold:MIN_AVG_TURNOVER = 200,000,000 (₹20 Cr daily turnover filter)
Example:Stock trading ₹35 Cr turnover ensures effortless entry and exit without market impact.
50-Day Average Volume (VOL50)Volume Baseline

The simple 50-session moving average of daily volume used as the baseline for volume anomalies.

Why it matters:Defines what "normal" activity looks like, allowing accurate detection of dry-ups and breakout spikes.
main.py Threshold:VOL50 = df["Volume"].rolling(50).mean()
Example:Baseline is 500,000 shares; today's 1.25M volume signals 2.5x institutional expansion.
52-Week High and LowAnnual Range

The highest and lowest price reached by the stock over the past 252 trading sessions (1 year).

Why it matters:Minervini rule: winning swing stocks emerge near 52-week highs, never from 52-week lows.
main.py Threshold:Close > 1.30 × LOW52W and Close > 0.75 × HIGH52W
Example:Stock is 45% above its 52-week low and within 12% of its 52-week high: qualifies for Stage 2.
Relative Strength vs IndexLeadership Rank

How strongly a stock performs compared to the benchmark NIFTY 50 index over 1 to 6 months.

Why it matters:True market leaders hold their ground or make new highs while the market index pulls back.
main.py Threshold:Sector Stage-2 filter and stock Stage-2 Minervini template
Example:NIFTY is flat over 3 months while COFORGE gains +18%: displays outstanding relative strength.
Stop LossRisk Control

A predetermined price level at which you automatically exit if the trade moves against you.

Why it matters:Guarantees that a bad trade remains an insignificant loss and never threatens your account.
main.py Threshold:Stop = max(Entry - 1.5 × ATR14, Entry × 0.93)
Example:Entry ₹1,000, Stop ₹950: Risk is strictly limited to ₹50 per share.
1.5x ATR StopDynamic Volatility Stop

Placing the initial stop loss at exactly 1.5 times the 14-day Average True Range below entry.

Why it matters:Avoids being shaken out by normal daily market noise while keeping risk strictly controlled.
main.py Threshold:ATR_STOP_MULTIPLIER = 1.5
Example:ATR14 is ₹30; 1.5 × 30 = ₹45 cushion below entry price.
Profit TargetExit Target

The predetermined price level where you book profits into momentum strength.

Why it matters:Locks in gains before predictable mean reversion or pullbacks occur.
main.py Threshold:Target = Entry + 2 × (Entry - Stop) (2.0R Multiple)
Example:Entry ₹500, Stop ₹475 (Risk ₹25); Target is ₹500 + 2×25 = ₹550.
On Deck / Near MissDiagnostic Watch

Stocks passing trend/setup/sector gates but failing volume or price-breakout confirmation. Not tradable yet, but prime candidates for next session's watch.

Why it matters:Identifies high-quality coiled stocks right before their breakout trigger, giving you advance situational awareness.
main.py Threshold:TREND_OK & SETUP_OK & sector_ok == True, but (BREAKOUT == False or VOL_SPIKE == False)
Action:Place on immediate radar for the next session; monitor for breakout price clearance and volume expansion.
RS Rating (Relative Strength)Diagnostic Metric

Percentile rank (1-99) of 1-year blended momentum: 0.4*ret_63d + 0.2*ret_126d + 0.2*ret_189d + 0.2*ret_252d against the universe.

Why it matters:Identifies market leaders with institutional sponsorship that tend to break out strongest.
main.py Threshold:Green pill: RS ≥ 90 | Blue pill: RS 80-89 | Gray: RS < 80 or n/a (universe < 30)
Action:Focus primary execution on stocks with RS ≥ 80 in Stage-2 uptrends.
Base Maturity / VCP contractionPattern Diagnostic

Measures volatility contraction within the active consolidation: base_start_idx = index of min(Low) over last 60 bars; days_in_base = len(df) - 1 - base_start_idx.

Why it matters:Tracks progressive volatility contraction (VCP stages) before an explosive expansion.
main.py Threshold:Count successive step-downs of 10-day rolling std of (High-Low)/Close; clamp 1..4 dots.
Example:4 dots = late-stage mature coil; 1-2 dots = early consolidation.
Leaders BoardCurated Radar

Top 10 relative strength leaders (1-99 RS) that also qualify in Stage-2 uptrend (TREND_OK).

Why it matters:Instantly highlights the strongest market leaders near or at new highs with Stage-2 alignment.
main.py Threshold:Top-10 highest RS rating satisfying TREND_OK (Stage-2), formatted as {symbol, rs_rating, close, dist_to_breakout, grade}.
Display:Collapsible strip above Near Buy table; horizontal scroll on mobile, compact grid on desktop.
Institutional Footprint (bulk/block + delivery)Informational Lens

Lens on institutional activity behind price and volume action using NSE bulk/block deal prints and delivery volume percentages.

Why it matters:Distinguishes genuine institutional accumulation from speculative intraday churn without affecting any trade signals or risk parameters.
main.py Threshold:Accumulation: (delivery_pct rising vs 5d-avg AND price flat/up) OR block_print/bulk_print True in last 3 sessions. Churn: volume ≥ 1.5x avg AND delivery_pct < 25% AND no inst print. Unknown: no data or stale deals.
Badges:🏦 green = accumulation, ⚠️ amber = churn, gray = unknown.
Event Radar / Gap RiskRisk Gating

Automated calendar tracking for corporate gap-events (quarterly earnings, promoter lock-in expiries, AGM, record dates, dividend payouts).

Entry Rule:BLOCKS any new swing entry if an event is scheduled within 7 days (EARNINGS_BLACKOUT_DAYS).
Open Position Rule:WARNS with a 🔔 badge if an event is within 3 days. Never auto-exits holdings (exit math, stop loss, and profit targets remain strictly unaltered).
Why it matters:Protects capital against overnight gap-risk (10-20% drops that plunge through hard stops) while maintaining strict trading rule integrity.
R-MultipleStandardized Metric

Expressing trade profit or loss in units of your initial risk (1R = Risk Budget).

Why it matters:Allows objective comparison of performance regardless of stock price or account size.
main.py Threshold:1R = ₹1,000 (1% on ₹1 Lakh). Target = +2R (+₹2,000). Max Stop = -1R (-₹1,000).
Example:A trade with ₹1,000 risk that earns ₹2,200 is an outcome of +2.2R.
Position SizeMoney Management

The exact number of shares purchased to align total trade risk with your portfolio risk budget.

Why it matters:Too many shares leads to account ruin; too few shares leads to negligible returns.
main.py Threshold:Quantity = floor( (Capital × RiskPct) / (Entry - Stop) )
Example:Risk budget ₹1,000, risk/unit ₹20 -> Quantity = 50 shares.
ExpectancySystem Edge

The expected average rupee payoff per trade over hundreds of historical trades.

Why it matters:Proves mathematically whether your strategy is profitable long-term or a coin toss.
main.py Threshold:Expectancy = (Win% × AvgWin) - (Loss% × AvgLoss) > ₹0
Example:50% wins × ₹2,000 - 50% losses × ₹1,000 = +₹500 positive edge per trade.
Win RateProbability

The percentage of total closed trades that generated positive realized profit.

Why it matters:Helps evaluate consistency. With a 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio, you only need a 35-40% win rate to be profitable.
main.py Threshold:Target Win Rate: 45% - 55%
Example:10 wins out of 20 trades = 50% win rate; produces +10R net account growth.
Profit FactorSystem Efficiency

Gross profits divided by gross losses across all closed trades.

Why it matters:A profit factor above 1.5 indicates a robust, high-performing trading edge.
main.py Threshold:Profit Factor = Gross Winning PnL / Gross Losing PnL (Target > 1.5)
Example:₹30,000 gross gains / ₹15,000 gross losses = 2.0 Profit Factor.
Max DrawdownPeak-to-Trough Risk

The largest percentage or rupee decline from an equity peak to a subsequent trough.

Why it matters:Protects psychology and prevents emotional breakdowns during normal losing streaks.
main.py Threshold:Strictly mitigated via 1% risk rule and 2% daily loss limit (Target < 8% account DD)
Example:Account grows to ₹115,000 then dips to ₹110,000 before reaching new highs: Drawdown is ₹5,000 (4.3%).
Trailing Stop (10 EMA)Dynamic Trend Exit

An exit rule where you ride big winners by holding until a session closes below the 10 EMA.

Why it matters:Allows exceptional market leaders to turn into 3R, 4R, or 5R multi-week trend runners.
main.py Threshold:Exit if daily Close < EMA10 once past initial target
Example:Stock runs from ₹500 to ₹650 without a single daily close under its rising 10 EMA line.

🎨 OUR RULES IN PICTURE FORM

Schematic vector blueprints of the core patterns and mechanical execution rules

1. Stage-2 Uptrend (Minervini Template)

  • Order of Averages: Current Price > SMA 50 > SMA 150 > SMA 200.
  • Ascending 200 SMA: The 200-day average must be trending upward for at least 50 days.
  • Proximity Rule: Price must be at least 30% above its 52-week low and within 25% of its 52-week high.

2. Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP)

  • Successive Contractions: Price pullbacks get shallower on each test (e.g. 15% -> 8% -> 3%).
  • Volume Dries Up: Trading volume shrinks steadily as sellers run out of stock.
  • Spring Loaded: The tightest contraction right below resistance requires minimal volume to trigger breakout.

3. Breakout Entry & Risk Setup

  • Decisive Close: Daily price finishes cleanly above the 20-day high resistance level.
  • Volume Confirmation: Session volume is at least 1.5x higher than the 50-day average.
  • Mathematical Asymmetry: Target is set at 2R reward against a 1R dynamic ATR stop.

4. Pullback Setup (2-Period RSI)

  • Stage 2 Context: Stock must already be established in an overarching uptrend.
  • Extreme Oversold: 2-period RSI drops below 10, signaling short-term panic selling.
  • Confirmation Candle: Buy only after the first green day where Close > previous Close.

5. Market Filter (NIFTY 50 Regime)

  • Market Direction: NIFTY 50 Index must satisfy Close > SMA50 > SMA200.
  • Defensive Protection: If index dips below moving averages, regime flips to Defensive.
  • Capital Safeguard: All setup scores are capped at 50, blocking aggressive entries.

6. Trade Management & Exits

  • Initial Risk: Fixed stop loss at 1.5x ATR (maximum 7% from entry price).
  • Breakeven Ratchet: When profit reaches +8%, immediately move stop to entry.
  • Trailing Exit: Trail along 10 EMA close or take profit at predefined 2R target.

🛡️ THE 10 RISK COMMANDMENTS

Non-negotiable trading principles that guarantee long-term survival and statistical edge

1

Risk Exactly 1% Per Trade

Never risk more than ₹1,000 per trade on a ₹100,000 capital base. 10 consecutive bad trades will only reduce your account by ~9.5%.

2

Maximum 2 Trades Per Day

Overtrading dilutes focus and causes poor entries. Strict 2-trade limit forces you to wait for the highest conviction Grade A+ setups.

3

Stop Trading After 2% Daily Loss

If two trades hit stops in one day, close the terminal and walk away. Emotional revenge trading is the #1 killer of swing trading accounts.

4

Never Average Down

Losers average losers. If a stock falls below your entry, it is proving your thesis wrong. Adding more capital to a losing trade is forbidden.

5

Never Revenge Trade

Losses are simply the wholesale cost of doing business. Accept them dispassionately, log them in your journal, and wait for the next setup.

6

Respect the 5-Day Time Stop

Real breakouts move swiftly. If a stock lingers around your entry for 5 trading sessions without follow-through, close it and free capital.

7

Check Earnings Dates Before Entry

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Straight answers to common questions about signals, defensive regimes, and emergency protocols

Why are there zero signals today? ▼
Our scanner applies 7 simultaneous filters (Stage 2 trend, 10-day coiling, 20-day high breakout, 1.5x volume spike, strong close in top 40%, sector trend confirmation, and NIFTY benchmark trend). Most days, no stock meets all 7 criteria. Having zero signals is normal and expected — it protects your capital from choppy, sub-par market environments.
Why does the dashboard say "Market: Defensive"? ▼
The O'Neil M-rule detected that the benchmark NIFTY 50 index (^NSEI) is trading below its 50-day or 200-day moving average, or the 50 SMA is below the 200 SMA. Statistical studies show that 75% of individual stocks follow the broader market direction. In a defensive market, all setup scores are capped at 50, and new buy signals are blocked to keep you safely in cash.
What does Grade B mean, can I trade it? ▼
Grade B means the setup scored between 60 and 69, or scored higher but failed the Sector confirmation filter. Grade B stocks are placed on the Watchlist so you can observe their consolidation. You should NOT buy Grade B setups. Only Grade A+ and Grade A setups provide the statistical edge necessary for profitable swing trading.
How fresh is the data displayed on the dashboard? ▼
The scanner runs automatically after the closing bell at 16:15 IST on all trading days. In addition, the Telegram listener runs every 5 minutes to sync trades, and this dashboard terminal refreshes its data from GitHub automatically every 5 minutes in the background.
What if a stock gaps down past my stop loss overnight? ▼
If bad news causes a stock to gap down below your stop loss at the 09:15 AM market open, exit immediately at market. Do NOT hope for an intraday bounce or average down. The 1% risk rule ensures that even a 2-3% gap-down loss will not cause catastrophic damage to your portfolio.
When should I NOT trade? ▼
Do not take new trades under three conditions: (1) When the Market Regime badge is red (Defensive); (2) Within 7 days of an upcoming quarterly earnings report for that company; or (3) If you have already taken 2 trades or hit your 2% loss limit for the day.
How do I change my dashboard access password? ▼
Generate the SHA-256 hash of your new password (using any online SHA-256 generator or browser console crypto.subtle.digest), and update the PASSWORD_HASH constant on line 6 of dashboard/app.js in your repository.
Where is my personal trading data stored? ▼
All your data is stored in your private GitHub repository in three JSON files: scan_results.json, open_positions.json, and trades.json. There are no third-party tracking databases or external servers involved. You have complete private ownership of your trading logs.
